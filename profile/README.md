# Hayuk!

### More play. Less hassle.

Hayuk is a social sports platform built to bring people together through play.

We're making it easier to find people, organize matches, and enjoy sports without the unnecessary complexity. From matchmaking and payments to future court bookings, everything is designed around one simple idea: **playing should be fun, not complicated.**

## What We're Building

- **Social Matchmaking** — Find your people, create matches, and play together.
- **Match Management** — Organize games, generate rounds, and keep everyone involved.
- **Seamless Payments** — Simple, transparent, and locally integrated payments.
- **Court Booking** — Bringing players and sports venues closer together in the future.

## What We Believe In

- **Play comes first.** Technology should make playing easier, not get in the way.
- **No hidden fees.** Transparent pricing, always.
- **Community over complexity.** Sports are better together.
- **Less noise, more fun.** No intrusive ads or unnecessary distractions.
- **Built for players.** Every decision starts with the player experience.

## Our Vision

We're starting with padel, but our ambitions go beyond a single sport.

Our vision is to build a connected recreational sports ecosystem where finding a game, meeting new people, and getting on the court feels effortless.

We're building the experience we'd love to use ourselves.
